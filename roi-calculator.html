Estimation & Pricing FAQs

How are the ~42% cost savings calculated? +
Savings are benchmarked against average US enterprise compensation packages ($145k-$185k base + 35% taxes, benefits, recruiting agency commissions, hardware, and dev licenses). CASPRO provides all-inclusive rates that eliminate fringe costs.
Can I adjust team size during an ongoing engagement? +
Yes. With our Dedicated Agile Squad model, you can scale squad capacity up or down with a standard two-week sprint notice, ensuring you only pay for the exact engineering bandwidth you need.
Does CASPRO provide binding quotes before we start? +
Yes. Following our complimentary 30-minute discovery call and architecture audit, we deliver a formal Statement of Work (SOW) with clear milestone deliverables, sprint schedules, and locked-in pricing.
