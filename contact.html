Enterprise Partnership FAQs

How quickly can CASPRO onboard a dedicated engineering squad? +
Most engagements kick off within 5 to 10 business days. Following our discovery session, we match pre-vetted senior engineers and solution architects tailored to your exact tech stack and sprint cadence.
How do you handle IP protection and enterprise security? +
All intellectual property (IP), code repositories, and documentation belong 100% to our clients from day one. We operate under strict bilateral NDAs and adhere to ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and GDPR standards.
What makes CASPRO's SAP GRC practice unique? +
Our SAP practice is led by seasoned GRC and S/4HANA security specialists with deep experience in SOX compliance, rulebook customization, emergency access management (Firefighter), and Segregation of Duties (SoD) remediation.
What commercial engagement models do you offer? +
We offer three flexible models: (1) Dedicated Agile Squads for continuous product engineering; (2) Fixed-Scope Deliveries for defined project milestones and audits; and (3) Strategic Time & Materials / TaaS for specialized consulting.
